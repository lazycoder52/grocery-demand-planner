## Historical Iteration & Figure Reconciliation

### Untraceable Figures Explanation
Earlier exploratory iterations reported -12.05% ($L=3$) and -14.54% ($L=7$) inventory reductions. These figures were generated using an early in-sample residual $\sigma$ buffer before the replenishment simulator was frozen with strictly out-of-sample forecast error buffers. Because they cannot be reproduced from frozen commits, they are declared invalid and are excluded from all repository documentation, tests, and metrics. Active measured figures are strictly traceable to `metrics/metrics.json`.
